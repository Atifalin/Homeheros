Our Story: Making Home Life Easier

We connect Canadian families with trusted household professionals for cleaning, cooking, pet care, and more—so you can reclaim your time for what truly matters.

Why We Exist

Life gets busy — we understand. That's why Home Heros is here to help with your household chores. Our goal is simple: Make it easy to find reliable, high-quality help — all from your phone. Whether it's cleaning, cooking, pet care, or more, we connect you with trusted professionals, so you can focus on what really matters.

Our Mission

‘To build a world where everyone has access to the services they love and have more time to spend with their family because every home deserves a hero’

Our Founders

Home Heros was founded by Utkarsh, Bavneet, and Garima after a coffee shop conversation about making everyday life easier for Canadian families. Inspired by personal experiences with the challenges of finding reliable household help, we created a platform that connects trusted professionals with families who need support. Our mission is to give people back their time for what truly matters while providing quality service they can depend on.
