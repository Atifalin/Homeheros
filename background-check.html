Background Check Required

To ensure the safety and trust of our community, all service providers must complete a background check before joining Home Heros.

What to Expect

The background check process includes:

  • Identity verification
  • Criminal record check
  • Employment history verification
  • Reference checks

The process typically takes 3-5 business days to complete.

What You'll Need

  • Valid government-issued photo ID
  • Social Insurance Number (SIN)
  • Current address information
  • Employment history for the past 5 years
